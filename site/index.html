A foldable iPhone in mirror-polished grade 5 titanium. Announced 9 September 2026. In stores 23 October.

Scroll to unfold. Held partly open. Motion is reduced. 5.2 mm, fully open.

Opened, 5.2 millimeters. Closed, a pocket.

iPhone 18 Pro reached stores on 18 September. Duo was announced the same morning and shipped on 23 October, which makes it the newest iPhone as of this note.

It folds on a vertical spine. Shut, it is a short slab: 84.1 by 117.8 millimeters, 11.3 millimeters thick, 254 grams. Open, the body widens to 164.6 millimeters and thins to 5.2. Apple calls that opened state the thinnest iPhone it has made. The drawing above is original. The figures are not.

02 — Metal

Two mirrors.

Night Sky finish

Grade 5 titanium, polished until it mirrors. The hinge cover is the exception: 3D-printed, micro-blasted, and made entirely of recycled titanium, so the spine stays matte while the frame throws a hard line of light.

Ceramic Shield 2 sits on the front. Apple rates its scratch resistance at three times the previous generation. Ceramic Shield covers the back. The seal is IP68, tested to 6 meters for 30 minutes.

04 — Display

One ratio, turned.

The outer panel is a 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR OLED, 1398 by 2034 pixels at 460 pixels per inch. Apple sizes it at about 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro.

The inner panel is 7.6 inches, 1878 by 2670 at 430 pixels per inch, with a nano-texture cover meant to cut glare and soften the crease. Apple sizes it at about 50 percent more area than iPhone 18 Pro Max.

Both share nearly the same aspect, so a page keeps its proportion when the phone opens. Measured as rectangles, the diagonals are 5.36 and 7.58 inches. The viewable area is smaller.

06 — Lens

Three stops.

Two rear cameras, not three. The range is still three honest steps: 13, 26, and 52 millimeters. The frame on the right zooms through them.

13 mm
48MP Fusion Ultra Wide, f/2.2, 120°. Macro lives here.
26 mm
48MP Fusion Main, f/1.6, sensor-shift stabilization.
52 mm
12MP optical-quality 2×, cropped from the Main.

Past that, digital zoom runs to 10×. The outer front camera is a 12-megapixel Center Stage at f/2.2. The inner one is an under-display FaceTime camera at f/1.8. The outer screen can preview a shot from the rear lenses, so the fold is part of the camera.
